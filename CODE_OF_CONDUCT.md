# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and technically honest.
- Critique ideas and designs, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give credit for work and disclose relevant sources.
- Prioritize safety when discussing electrical and manufacturing decisions.
- Accept corrections gracefully and help resolve disagreements with evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate misinformation, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile contact options. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential
to the extent practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
